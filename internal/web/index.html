Placement

Records = (size + overhead) × records × RF × (1 + growth); on device, × (1 + fragmentation) × compression. Primary index in RAM = 64 B per copy; on flash, 4 KiB sprigs holding 64 entries at the fill share (partition-tree-sprigs rounded up to a power of two, × 4096 partitions × RF), with 10 B of RAM each. Secondary indexes = entries × 14 B × sparseness × RF; set indexes = 16 B per copy. CPU = (reads/s + writes/s) ÷ transactions per vCPU. Device IOPS = writes/s × RF × defrag + reads/s × (1 − cache hit) with records on device, plus one flash index read per read and a read and a write per replica write with the index on flash. Growth applies to the records, not to the throughput. Nodes per resource = demand ÷ what a node offers at its ceiling on the nodes left after the planned loss, never fewer than RF+1, rounded up so that every rack holds as many nodes.
